What we do

Project work, and the long term

Three kinds of project work, plus the ongoing support that keeps it all running afterwards.

Create

A brand that looks right everywhere people find you

Your place looks better in person than it does on a screen. Closing that gap is design work — and it doesn't always stop at the screen.

  • Photography — spaces, product, team, events
  • Social media management and content
  • Graphic design — logos, menus, print
  • Website design and build
  • Email campaigns and local SEO
  • Paid digital advertising
  • Physical pieces when a job calls for them — banners, signs, decals

Real photos of your actual business, not a stock library.

Configure

Tech setup that finally talks to itself

Often you don't need new software. You need someone who knows how to set up the software you're already paying for.

  • POS systems — Toast, Square, Clover
  • E-commerce — Shopify, WooCommerce
  • Booking and reservation platforms
  • Payment processing and accounting
  • CRM and email marketing tools
  • Integrations between all of the above

Your systems, doing what you thought you bought them to do.

Build

Software built for one business — yours

When the product you need doesn't exist, we write it. Real code, built around the way you already work — not a workaround, and not another subscription that almost fits.

  • Web apps and internal dashboards
  • Booking and scheduling around your rules
  • Client portals and customer tools
  • Automation for the manual stuff
  • iOS and Android apps
  • APIs between systems that don't connect

Ten years of production code behind every build.

The long term

Ongoing support

Projects end. This doesn't. Most small businesses don't have an IT person and don't need a full-time one — they need someone who already knows their setup to pick up the phone.

We deal with the vendors. Your POS company, your processor, your internet provider. We sit in the hold queue instead of you.

Network & wifi

Coverage that reaches the whole floor, and a guest network kept separate from the one your card reader runs on.

Hardware

Workstations, tablets, printers, card readers, and displays — sourced, set up, and replaced before they fail mid-shift.

Email & accounts

Business email on your own domain, shared calendars and files, and access added or removed as staff come and go.

Security & backups

A password manager your team will actually use, two-factor where it counts, and backups tested by restoring from them.

Documentation

Written down as we go, so your business isn't dependent on any one person remembering how it works. Us included.

When something breaks

Remote help first, on site when it needs hands. You're talking to the person who set it up, not a ticket queue.
