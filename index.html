Questions You're Probably Asking

Honest answers about how we work and what you can expect

Where are you located? Do you work remotely?

+

We work with businesses nationwide. Most of our work can be done remotely—consultations over video call, development work from anywhere, and digital deliverables sent electronically.

For services that require on-site work (like professional photography or in-person training), we travel to you. Location isn't a barrier to working together.

Can you help if I already have a website/system in place?

+

Yes. We can work with what you already have—whether that's updating an existing website, fixing issues with your current setup, integrating new tools, or building additions to your current systems.

Not everything needs to be rebuilt from scratch. Sometimes the best solution is improving what's already working.

What's your pricing structure?

+

Every engagement is different, so pricing is customized based on your specific needs and scope.

Our approach:

  • Free initial consultation to understand your requirements
  • Fixed-price quotes for one-time builds—no hourly billing surprises
  • Monthly retainers for ongoing work like social media management or IT support
  • Clear deliverables and timeline outlined upfront
  • No hidden fees or change orders unless you change the scope

Once we understand what you need, we'll give you an exact quote. Simple builds cost less, complex custom software costs more—we'll be transparent about pricing.

How long does it take?

+

Timelines vary based on what you need. During your free consultation, we'll give you a realistic timeline for your specific situation.

For one-time builds:

  • Discovery & Planning: We dive deep into your needs and map out the solution
  • Development: Regular check-ins keep you informed as we build
  • Testing & Refinement: Your feedback shapes the final product
  • Launch & Training: We ensure you're confident using your new tools

For ongoing services: Social media management, IT support, and marketing work happens on a continuous basis with regular deliverables.

We don't overpromise timelines to win business. If something will take 8 weeks, we'll tell you 8 weeks upfront—not "2 weeks" only to miss the deadline later.

What if I need changes or updates later?

+

Your business changes, and your digital tools should change with it. We're available for updates, new features, or modifications whenever you need them.

For one-off changes, we can quote them individually. For ongoing needs, monthly retainer arrangements often make more sense. Either way, we're here when you need us.

Do I need to provide anything to get started?

+

Just come to the consultation ready to talk about your business and what you're trying to accomplish. We'll guide you through everything else.

If you have existing assets (logos, photos, access to current systems), great—we'll work with them. If not, we can create what's needed or help you get access to what we need.

Why should I choose you over a bigger agency?

+

Honestly, bigger agencies have their strengths—they can handle massive enterprise needs and have dedicated departments for everything.

Choose us if you value:

  • Direct communication: Work directly with the developers building your solution
  • Custom-built solutions: We write code from scratch, not template customization
  • Engineering approach: We solve problems methodically, focused on what actually works
  • Honest feedback: We'll tell you when your idea needs refinement or when there's a better approach
  • Flexibility: Need to adjust course? We can pivot without committee approvals
