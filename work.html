What the work looks like

Three small businesses, written up the way we'd explain them to you on a call: the problem, what we did about it, and what changed.

Systems & integration

Untangling a restaurant's order flow

Problem

Orders arriving from three delivery apps on three separate tablets, a POS that didn't know about any of them, and a menu that had to be updated four times every time a price changed. Staff were fixing errors instead of serving people.

What we did
  • Set up and configured Toast from the ground up
  • Routed DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub into it
  • Made one menu the source of truth for every platform
  • Trained the team and left written documentation
  • Stayed on for day-to-day IT support
Result

One screen, one menu, far fewer wrong orders — and staff back on the floor instead of behind a tablet.

Photography & social

Getting a historic inn to look like itself online

Problem

A property full of character, represented online by a handful of dated photos and a social account that went quiet for weeks at a time. Guests couldn't see what made it worth booking.

What we did
  • Shot the property in the light it actually looks best in
  • Built a content plan and a posting rhythm we keep to
  • Took over posting, captions, and replies
  • Made the look consistent across every platform
Result

An online presence that matches the room you walk into — and reaches guests who care about that.

Design, sourced and installed

Opening day for a therapy practice

Problem

A practice about to open with nothing on the building to say so, and a window problem with no obvious answer: the offices were visible from the sidewalk, but they didn't want to give up the daylight or the view out.

What we did
  • Designed a custom grand opening banner for the front of the building
  • Designed a matching sign for the porch
  • Sourced both through printers we trust and approved the proofs
  • Designed branded window decals in perforated vinyl — solid from the street, see-through from inside
  • Measured, mounted, and installed everything on site
Result

Private offices without losing the light or the view out — and a practice that read as open and established on day one.
