The things people ask before they email

Straight answers about cost, timing, and how we work.

It depends on the scope, so we quote per project rather than publish rates that wouldn't apply to your situation.

  • The first consultation is free
  • One-time builds get a fixed price, not hourly billing
  • Continuing work like social or IT support is billed monthly at an agreed rate
  • Deliverables and dates are written down before we start
  • The price changes only if you change the scope

A simple build costs less than complex custom software. We'll tell you which one you're asking for.

You'll get a real timeline on the first call, once we know the scope. A build usually moves through discovery, development, testing with your feedback, then launch and training.

Continuing services — social media, IT support, marketing — run month to month with deliverables on a set schedule.

We don't shorten estimates to win the job. If it's eight weeks, we say eight weeks in the proposal rather than two weeks and an apology later.

Rebuilding from scratch is sometimes the right call and often isn't. We'll say which we think it is.

Everything else works anywhere. Websites, design, custom software, and system setup get done remotely, with consultations over video and files delivered electronically. Plenty of our clients we've never stood in the same room as.
