Process

Four steps, in this order, every time

You always know which one you're in and what happens next.

Step 01

Talk it through

A free call about your business and what's not working. No pitch. Sometimes the answer is a setting you already own, and we'll tell you that.

Step 02

Get a written scope

Exactly what we'll build, when you'll have it, and a fixed price. If the scope doesn't change, the number doesn't either.

Step 03

Watch it get built

Regular check-ins with real progress to look at. You give feedback while things are still easy to change.

Step 04

Launch and hand over

We put it live, train whoever uses it, and stay on call for 30 days. Need us after that, we're still here.
